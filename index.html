📖 Acronyms, Metrics & Signal Percentages Explained

Clear definitions for STH MVRV & SOPR, MVRV Band, PVSRA, SFP, Liquidation Zones, Power Law, MWA, and ST / MT / LT win percentages.

Reference Guide
STH MVRV, STH Cost Basis & STH-SOPR Bands

Tracks coins moved within the last 155 days (Short-Term Holders). STH Cost Basis shows average recent buyer entry; MVRV-STH and STH-SOPR measure whether recent coins are spent/held in profit or loss, with standard deviation bands (+2.0sd to -1.5sd) marking overextension vs. capitulation/support[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

MVRV & MVRV Band (Market Value to Realized Value)

Compares current Market Value (spot price × circulating supply) against Realized Value (each coin valued at the price when it last moved on-chain, reflecting aggregate cost basis). < 1.2 = undervalued/accumulation band; 1.2–2.4 = fair/neutral band; > 2.4 = overvalued/euphoria distribution zone.

PVSRA (Price, Volume, Support/Resistance Analysis)

Evaluates candlestick spread (high-low range), close position, and volume relative to a 20-bar average. High/climax volume (≥2.0× average) or rising volume (≥1.5× average) helps distinguish institutional absorption/stopping volume from climactic distribution or weak moves.

Short-Term (ST), Medium-Term (MT) & Long-Term (LT) %

Represents the signal win/accuracy rate for that time horizon: ST (1–7 days), MT (1–4 weeks), and LT (1–6 months+). They reflect historical/modeled accuracy rather than a guarantee on any single trade.

💥 Larger Liquidation Zones (Long vs. Short Clusters)

Price bands where concentrated leveraged long or short positions face forced liquidation. Sweeps into downside long-liquidation pockets can accelerate drops or trigger liquidity bounces, while moves into upside short-liquidation zones can fuel short squeezes or supply rejections.

Power Law & MWA (50 / 100 / 200D / 200W)

Bitcoin's Power Law curve models long-term logarithmic fair value since Jan 3, 2009. 50 / 100 / 200D MA and 200W MA provide short-, medium-, and macro-cycle moving average context.
