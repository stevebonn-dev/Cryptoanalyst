G indicator scorecard

G is a regime-switching composite (MACD, RSI, Bollinger %B, Donchian, ADX, Kaufman efficiency, volume confirmation, a high-volatility damper, weighted MAs, an overextension guard and taker-buy CVD). Binance keeps only about 30 days of derivatives history, so top-trader, crowd and perp-flow data cannot be backtested; it is scored live as a confirm/conflict check instead. Per asset, its entry threshold is chosen on the first 24 months and tested on the last 12. Ratings use only the out-of-sample year. Each live signal is scored 0–100 after its hold period, and poor scores tighten that asset's threshold (rate α).
