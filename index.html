G indicator scorecard

G is a regime-switching composite (VWAP and 60-day volume-profile value area are optional features that the backtest switches on or off per asset; MACD, RSI, Bollinger %B, Donchian, ADX, Kaufman efficiency, volume confirmation, a high-volatility damper, weighted MAs, an overextension guard and taker-buy CVD). Binance keeps only about 30 days of derivatives history, so top-trader, crowd and perp-flow data cannot be backtested; it is scored live as a confirm/conflict check instead. Per asset, its entry threshold is chosen on the first 24 months and tested on the last 12. Four optional features (BTC relative strength, squeeze breakout, Supertrend, Fibonacci confluence) are added only if they raise the in-sample score by 3 or more points; each card shows the out-of-sample score with and without them. Fibonacci levels (retracements 38.2–78.6% and 127.2/161.8% extensions from the latest 90-day swing) appear on the chart and in the zones only when the backtest shows an in-sample gain of 3 or more points with no out-of-sample loss; the Fib button forces them on or off. Ratings use only the out-of-sample year. Each live signal is scored 0–100 after its hold period, and poor scores tighten that asset's threshold (rate α).
