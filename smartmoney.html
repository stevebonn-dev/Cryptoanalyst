📖 Methodology & Honest Limitations

What this is: a composite of observable large-player footprints — taker CVD & taker ratio (Binance klines field 9), funding regime, Coinbase premium, stablecoin supply momentum, CFTC COT asset-manager net (CME BTC, weekly), plus 30-day OI/top-trader positioning. Each feature maps to [−1,+1]; the composite is a learned weighted average; zones ±0.15/±0.35 define emerging/confirmed pressure and transitions fire alerts.
What this is NOT: identification of actual market-maker inventory (Wintermute, Jump, Cumberland… are delta-neutral and internally invisible; labeled-wallet data is paid and exchange-internal flow never appears on-chain). COT leveraged-fund shorts are largely hedged basis trades — that's why the asset-manager leg is used. Binance OI/top-trader history is limited to 30 days, so those features are recent-only and the composite renormalizes when a source is missing.
Self-improvement: online Hedge updates (η=0.06) on matured 7-day calls, per-regime buckets with n≥30 gate, weight clamps [0.25×,3×], 3% shrinkage to priors, feature quarantine, Brier-drift alarm, and a no-look-ahead walk-forward. With ~700 daily samples and overlapping horizons, effective sample size is small — expect modest edges, not magic. Nothing here is financial advice.
